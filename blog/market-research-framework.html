A Simple Market Research Framework for Small Businesses

A repeatable starting point for reviewing customers, competitors, positioning and growth opportunities.

Living articleThis is a polished framework prepared for the portfolio. Add verified examples, screenshots, source notes and personal observations here as the evidence becomes available.

Define the decision

Start with the decision you need to make: choose a market, refine an offer, enter a location or improve customer acquisition.

Map the customer

Describe the customer problem, alternatives, buying triggers and information that helps people trust an offer.

Study competitors

Review positioning, offers, pricing signals, messaging, reviews and customer experience. Look for patterns and gaps, not just names.

Turn evidence into action

The output should be a set of choices: who to target, what to say, what to test and what evidence would change your mind.

Keep the framework reusable

A good research process can be repeated as the market changes. Document assumptions, sources and decisions so future updates are easier.
